The rule

Nothing here sends. There is no send path in the repo.

Scout · Desk · Chaser · Closer └─► GHOST (the voice) └─► outbox.csv status = draft └─► DAVID reads each one — approves or kills └─► draft.sh ──► a Gmail DRAFT in his Gmail └─► he presses Send. nothing else does └─► bizdave.py ──► BizDave: "drafts waiting"

Agents write rows and stop. Approved rows become real Gmail drafts that sit in David's Gmail until he presses Send himself. send.sh was deleted from the tree on 8 September — removed, not disabled — and no flag or variable brings it back.

Enforced in three places, so losing one opens no hole: no script calls a send tool; the scheduled permission profile denies sending and drafting, so a scheduled agent writes CSV and stops; the drafting profile allows only create_draft.

LinkedIn is read-only to every agent. The 2,558-message archive and the 7,246-person connection graph are read. Nothing opens linkedin.com, clicks, or types — LinkedIn drafts are listed for David to paste by hand.
