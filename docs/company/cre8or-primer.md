# Cre8or — company primer

Source: internal decks and docs in `~/Attachments Cre8or` (Cre8orOS, "Logic to Cre8orOS",
"The Problem We Solve", "Cre8or & Aggregators", Partner Deck 11/24). Distilled 2026-09-03.
This is the shared factual base for every GTM argument in this repo.

## What Cre8or is

A **vertically integrated beauty & personal-care manufacturer with an AI operating
system bolted to it.** Not a software company that partners with factories — Cre8or
owns the factories, and built the software to make on-demand production actually work.

Two halves, and the pitch only lands when both are present:

**The physical half.** Owned manufacturing and filling/packaging facilities, 30+ years
of formulation data, in-house R&D and unique formulations, packaging design, regulatory
and compliance handling (incl. MoCRA), logistics and fulfillment. One vendor from
concept to consumer — no coordinating a formulator, a filler, a packager, and a 3PL.

**The digital half — Cre8orOS / Cre8orST (Smart Trading).** Connects the factory to
the point of sale: AI product ideation and design, product lifecycle management,
demand planning, inventory optimisation, POS and 3PL API integrations, a Shopify app,
and a unified dashboard. The stated goal is a "DTC trifecta" — production, inventory,
and sales on one data model.

## The core commercial claim

Beauty brands lose money at both ends of the inventory problem: **overstocking**
(capital dead in unsold units) and **stockouts** (demand you can't serve). The industry
forces this because traditional cosmetic manufacturing has high MOQs and long lead times,
so you must guess demand months ahead and buy against the guess.

Cre8or's answer is **just-in-time, on-demand manufacturing with grow-as-you-go volume:
production runs from 12 units to 50,000 units without changing suppliers**, with reorder
levels driven by live POS data rather than a forecast made a quarter earlier.

That single capability — *start at 12, scale to 50,000, same supplier, same SKU, decided
by real sales* — is the wedge. Most of this repo's strategy work is about finding the
buyers for whom that is worth the most money.

## What this means for indirect / retail GTM

The people who benefit most from *12-to-50,000, driven by live sales* are the people
with **volatile, event-shaped, or unproven demand** and **an audience they already own**:
a tour, a launch, a team's season, a creator's drop, a retailer's private-label test.
They have the demand side solved and the supply side broken. Cre8or is the supply side.
