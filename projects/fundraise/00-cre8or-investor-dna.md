# Cre8or — investor evaluation brief

Single source of truth for how Cre8or is described to investors, and the criteria any
prospective investor is scored against. The interactive investor evaluator in the
fundraise artifact is fed from this file — keep them in sync.

## What Cre8or is

A beauty and personal-care manufacturing platform with an AI operating system on top.
Cre8orOS connects live point-of-sale demand signal directly to production scheduling.

**The wedge:** on-demand production from 12 units to 50,000 units, same supplier, no
forecast, no upfront inventory. The next generation of brands will not build supply
chains; they will plug into one.

**Customers:** beauty brands, agencies, licensors, merch aggregators and creators who
build product lines for their own clients.

## The related-party question — pre-empt it, never bury it

Because the plant sits with a co-founder rather than the company, every serious investor
will ask four things. Have answers ready before diligence:

1. What contractually binds the factory to Cre8or — exclusivity, guaranteed capacity, term?
2. Transfer pricing: the ~50% GM depends on an intercompany price a related party sets.
   Benchmark it against third-party contract-manufacturer quotes.
3. What happens if the co-founder exits or sells the plant?
4. Does Cre8or capture the manufacturing margin, or does it accrue to a shareholder personally?

`[OPEN]` A written supply agreement with term, defined pricing and exclusivity is the single
highest-value document to have before diligence. It is the difference between "privileged
access" and "a handshake."

## The three pitch directions

Cre8or is three companies at once. Which one you lead with decides whether an investor
engages. Never send all three.

### A — CONSUMER / BRAND PLATFORM
For consumer and CPG funds who back brands and already know MOQ, lead-time and deadstock pain.
Lead with: brands launch and scale without inventory risk; 12-unit minimums make product lines
possible that a 10,000-unit MOQ makes impossible. Proof: the 65,000-unit demand-signal story.

### B — VERTICAL AI / COMMERCE INFRASTRUCTURE
For retail-tech, supply-chain and applied-AI funds. Lead with: Cre8orOS is the software layer
over an industry that still runs on spreadsheets and forecasts; POS signal to production
scheduling is the loop nobody else closes. The plant is distribution and proof, not the business.
Best framing where the asset-light balance sheet is an advantage.

### C — INDUSTRIAL / ASSET-COMFORTABLE CAPITAL
For family offices, industrial strategics, corporate venture arms with manufacturer LPs, and
banks. Lead with: dedicated capacity, real COGS, real margins, real customers, patient horizon.
This is the only group that reads owned production as durability rather than as risk.

## Scoring criteria for a new investor

Score 1-5 on each. Anything averaging under 3 is not worth an intro request.

1. **Sector fit** — do they back consumer/beauty/CPG, or the software layer over physical industries?
2. **Stage fit** — can they write into a $8M Series A? Growth funds with revenue minimums we do not
   clear yet are a "later" not a "no".
3. **Asset tolerance** — can they live with founder-adjacent manufacturing, or will it be fatal?
4. **Strategic value beyond capital** — do they bring US retail distribution, brand customers, or
   manufacturing credibility?
5. **Reachability** — is there a warm path? Eddie Krule (Stout) is the primary one.
