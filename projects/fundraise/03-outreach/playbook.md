# Outreach playbook — Eddie Krule referral motion

All outreach sends from david@cre8orglobal.com and names Eddie Krule of Stout as the
referrer. Eddie gave standing permission on 26 Apr 2026 ("Lets catch-up" in reply to an
explicit request for intros to strategic, growth and family-office contacts).

## Rule zero — two different asks, never mixed

**Ask Eddie to forward** for anyone in his own network. A forwarded intro from a Managing
Director at Stout converts far better than a cold email that merely cites him.
**Send direct, citing Eddie** only where Eddie has no relationship. Citing someone as a
referrer when they did not actually make the intro is a one-time trick and it burns him.

Before any batch goes out, send Eddie the list and let him mark which ones he will forward.

## Sequence 3 — NEW, via Eddie's forward

For firms on the expanded target list where Eddie has a relationship. Send Eddie a single
email containing a ready-to-forward blurb so his effort is one click. Never ask him to
write the pitch himself.

## Sequence 4 — NEW, direct with attribution

For firms Eddie does not know. Opens by naming him and the list he sent, then gets to the
specific reason this firm and not another. Personalisation is the whole game: name a
portfolio company of theirs and why Cre8or is adjacent to it.

## Cadence

Day 0 send. Day 4 short bump in-thread. Day 11 final value-add (a metric, a customer win,
a relevant market note) then stop. Three touches, then the name moves to a quarterly
update list rather than being chased.

Never run more than 15 new outreach emails in a week. The bottleneck is the follow-up
conversations, not the sending.

## Tracking

Status values used in the artifact and in this repo:
`new` -> `queued` -> `sent` -> `replied` -> `meeting` -> `diligence` -> `passed` / `committed`

A name that reaches `passed` keeps its reason. Reasons cluster, and the cluster tells us
which of the three pitch directions is failing.
