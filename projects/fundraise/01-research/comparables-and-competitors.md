# Comparables, competitors, and the investors who already funded this model

`[status 2026-09-08]` The highest-value research output of the fundraise. Knowing who has
already written a cheque for on-demand / POS-driven production is worth more than any
amount of thesis matching.

## ⚠ Keychain — the direct competitor

New York. AI CPG manufacturing platform. Launched **KeychainOS**, a factory operating
system, in **August 2025**, and has since **expanded into beauty and personal care**.

**~$78M raised across five rounds:**

| Round | Amount | Lead | Also in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seed, Nov 2023 | $18M | Lightspeed | BoxGroup, Afore, SV Angel |
| Series A, Nov 2024 | $15M | BoxGroup | General Mills, Schreiber Foods |
| Feb 2025 | $5M | Bright Pixel Capital | |
| Series B, Aug 2025 | $30M | Wellington Management (Private Climate) | BoxGroup |
| Nov 2025 | $10M | W23 Global (Tesco, Ahold Delhaize, Woolworths, Empire, Shoprite) | |

Other named backers: Hershey, Rich's.

**Two consequences for the raise:**
1. Every investor in the priority list below has seen this pitch. The deck needs a clear
   answer to *"why not Keychain."* The honest one is beauty depth plus dedicated capacity,
   versus a marketplace model.
2. **Lightspeed and BoxGroup may be conflicted.** Establish that before spending a meeting.

## Other live comparables

- **Resonance Companies** (NY) — "Physical AI" for zero-inventory, no-minimum apparel. Ran
  its **own factory 2017–2020 as a living lab**, now routes orders across factory nodes.
  That is the exact Cre8or arc, already walked. ~$48.8M raised. Chaired by FirstMark's
  Lawrence Lenihan.
- **Prose** (Brooklyn) — custom haircare manufactured per order; pitch was that "the
  traditional FMCG model based on upfront mass production is coming to an end."
  Series B $18M led by **Insight**, with **Forerunner, Lerer Hippeau**, ISAI, Correlation.
- **Pietra** (NY) — creators launch lines at the lowest MOQs. **a16z** seed and 2023 round,
  **Founders Fund** led the $15M Series A; **M13** listed. `[CAUTION]` Cre8or has a live
  commercial relationship with Pietra — treat as competitor and counterparty at once.
- **Gelato** (Oslo) — software connecting idle production capacity. **$240M led by Insight**
  with SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Goldman Sachs AM, Dawn. $1B+ valuation.
- **Blanka** (Vancouver) — private-label-as-a-service beauty, launch a brand for under $200.
  $2M seed led by Dundee Venture Capital.
- **The/Studio** (LA) — small-batch custom manufacturing via vetted factories, sweet spot
  ~100 units. $11M Series A led by Ignition Partners.
- **Unspun** (Oakland) — on-demand 3D weaving micro-factory, zero inventory. $14M Series A
  led by **Lowercarbon**.
- **Rodinia Generation** (Copenhagen) — microfactories making clothes only in quantities
  bought. €3M from EIFO and **Climentum**.
- **Relocalize** (Montreal) — autonomous microfactories, cold-chain, on demand. $18.1M total.
- **Printify** ($45M led by **Index**, with H&M Group) and **Printful** ($130M from Bregal
  Sagemount) — merged Nov 2024.
- **Function of Beauty** — $150M from **L Catterton**, Dec 2020.

`[CAUTIONARY]` **Fast Radius** went bankrupt 2022; **Shapeways** filed Chapter 7 in 2024.
Expect to be asked why on-demand manufacturing platforms fail. Have the answer ready — both
were capacity marketplaces without owned demand signal.

## Priority: investors with repeat exposure to this exact model

1. **Insight Partners** — funded Prose AND Gelato. The only firm to back the no-upfront-inventory
   thesis twice. Too large to lead $8M; start the Series B relationship now. Arjun Mehta.
2. **BoxGroup + Lightspeed** — three rounds into Keychain. Highest conviction in the category,
   and the most likely conflict. Check first.
3. **Forerunner** — Prose and Curology. Has seen made-to-order beauty play out twice.
4. **FirstMark** — led Crisp; Lenihan chairs Resonance. Amish Jani owns commerce infrastructure
   and vertical software.
5. **8VC** — holds **Alloy.ai**, which connects POS, inventory and shipment data for CPG supply
   chains. Closest analogue to Cre8orOS anywhere. Pitch: "Alloy plus a factory." Jake Medwell.
6. **Lerer Hippeau** — Prose, plus Augmentir and P-1 AI in industrial AI, plus 10Beauty.
7. **W23 Global** — retailer-LP fund (Tesco, Ahold Delhaize, Woolworths). Its LPs *own the POS*.
   Nobody else pitching Cre8or will have thought of this angle.
8. **Lowercarbon / Climentum / Congruent** — the overproduction-waste framing, which is true
   here rather than retrofitted.
9. **Strategic CPG money already in the category**: General Mills, Hershey, Schreiber, Rich's
   (all Keychain), H&M Group (Printify), L Catterton (Function of Beauty).

## Lead-capable digital-industrial firms at this round size

- **Ironspring Ventures** (Ty Findley, Peter Holt) — Prokeep, Copia, plus a stealth
  design-to-manufacturability platform. Thesis bullseye.
- **Third Prime** — **First Resonance** (ION factory OS) and **Bops** (supply-chain
  intelligence for manufacturers). "Profitable in an unglamorous vertical" is their language.
- **Construct Capital** (Dayna Grayson, Rachel Holt) — leads Series A in manufacturing software.
- **Tribeca Venture Partners** — initial checks $1–6M, **leads rounds of $3–15M**. Structurally
  one of the best fits on the board for an $8M A. ShopKeep (retail POS) is the precedent.
- **Emergence Capital** — physical-world AI; project44, Drishti. Jake Saper. High bar, real fit.
- **Eclipse Ventures** — strongest thesis language ("the physical economy") but a hardware-skewed
  portfolio; frame Cre8orOS as the factory operating layer.
- **Bain Capital Ventures** — commerce practice explicitly names inventory forecasting.
  Scott Friend (commerce, NY), Ajay Agarwal (supply chain/logistics).
