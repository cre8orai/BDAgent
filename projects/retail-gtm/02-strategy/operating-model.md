# Operating model — how a partner programme actually runs

**Status:** ⚪ Draft frame. Validate against ops once research A2–A4 land.

## The lifecycle of one partner-led product line

```
1. Brief          Partner brings a client, an IP, an audience, a date.
2. Concept        Cre8or AI ideation + formulation shortlist against the brief.
3. Spec & approve Formulation, packaging, artwork. Licensor approval gate (segments A/C).
4. Compliance     Safety, MoCRA / regional registration, labelling.
5. Pilot run      Small run — seeding, sampling, influencer, pre-order.
6. Launch         Partner sells through their own channel; POS connected to Cre8orOS.
7. Replenish      Reorder driven by live sell-through, not forecast.
8. Wind-down      Line ends without surplus. This is the promise; it must be true.
```

Steps 3 and 4 are where deals die and timelines slip. `[OPEN]` Get real durations for
each step from ops (A2) — the partner's whole decision rests on whether step 1 → 6 fits
inside their event calendar.

## What Cre8or owns vs. what the partner owns

| | Cre8or | Partner |
|---|---|---|
| IP / licence | — | ✅ |
| Creative direction | supports | ✅ |
| Formulation & R&D | ✅ | approves |
| Packaging & artwork production | ✅ | approves |
| Regulatory & compliance | ✅ `[OPEN]` see E1 | — |
| Manufacturing | ✅ | — |
| Inventory & fulfilment | ✅ | — |
| Demand data / POS feed | ✅ (system) | ✅ (source) |
| Sales & retail placement | — | ✅ |
| Customer relationship | — | ✅ |

The clean line: **Cre8or owns everything from formulation to the customer's doorstep.
The partner owns everything from the audience to the sale.** Ambiguity here is where
partner relationships fail, so it is worth stating this bluntly in the agreement.

## What partners need from us to succeed

- **A named person.** These are relationship businesses; a portal is not a substitute.
- **Speed commitments they can plan against.** A licensor books venue retail months out.
- **Sales collateral they can put their own logo on** — they are selling this to *their*
  client, and will not forward a Cre8or deck.
- **Visibility.** `[OPEN]` Does a partner get a Cre8orOS seat to see their lines'
  production and stock status? A partner-facing view is a genuine moat and a real
  product commitment. Decide deliberately, not by default.
