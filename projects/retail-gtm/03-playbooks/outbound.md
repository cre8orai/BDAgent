# Outbound motion

**Status:** ⚪ Not started. Sequence after target list exists.

## Approach

Named-account, event-anchored. Not volume outbound — the universe of real targets in
segments A–E is small enough to work by hand, and each one deserves a specific reason
for contact.

**The trigger to lead with:** a partner's announced tour, season, launch, or client win
with a date attached. The message is about *their* calendar, not our capability.
