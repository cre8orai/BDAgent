# Pitch narrative

**Status:** ⚪ Skeleton. Fill after `02-strategy/positioning.md` is finalised.

## The 30-second version

> You already have the audience. What you don't have is a way to make beauty products
> for it without betting on volume six months early. We manufacture on demand — twelve
> units or fifty thousand, same line, same price structure, driven by what actually
> sells. You launch more lines, and the ones that miss cost you almost nothing.

## The meeting arc

1. **Their world** — what they launched last year, how the volume decisions got made,
   what was left over. Let them describe the pain; don't assert it.
2. **The category gap** — why beauty is the obvious extension they haven't made, and
   what has blocked it. `[OPEN]` needs B4's MOQ/lead-time comparison.
3. **The mechanism** — on-demand manufacturing, POS-connected replenishment. Plain
   language. One diagram at most.
4. **Proof it's real** — owned factories, 30 years of formulation, compliance handled.
   This is where manufacturing heritage belongs.
5. **Their numbers** — margin per line, lines per year, cost of a miss before and after.
6. **A specific first programme** — name their actual upcoming event or client. The ask
   is one line, not a partnership.

## Rules

- Ask for **one product line**, not a partnership agreement. Partnerships are signed
  after a programme works, not before.
- Bring their event calendar to the meeting. Specificity is the whole pitch.
- Never present the AI as the product.
