# Retail GTM — selling through agencies and intermediaries

**Status:** 🟡 Scaffolded. Research not yet started.
**Owner:** David
**Started:** 2026-09-03

## The question this project answers

Cre8or's direct motion sells to brand owners one at a time. This project designs the
**indirect motion**: how we sell *to* agencies, licensors, merch aggregators, and other
intermediaries, so that *they* put Cre8or-made product lines in front of their own clients.

One partner signed can produce many product lines. That leverage is the point.

## The shape of the deal

```
   Cre8or                    The intermediary               Their client / audience
   ──────                    ────────────────               ──────────────────────
   formulation               owns the IP or the             the artist, the team,
   manufacturing             client relationship            the retailer, the fans
   packaging + compliance    owns creative + audience
   JIT inventory OS          owns retail placement
   fulfilment                takes the margin
```

**Worked example (the canonical one):** Fanatics holds licensing rights and a merch
operation. They buy from Cre8or and stand up a Taylor Swift beauty line for a stadium
tour. Cre8or never touches the artist relationship. Fanatics never touches a factory.

Why that example is the right one to build the pitch around: a tour has **hard dates and
unknowable per-city demand.** Guess high and you eat pallets of dead cosmetics after the
last show. Guess low and you leave the highest-margin nights of the year unserved.
Cre8or's 12-to-50,000 on-demand model is a direct answer to exactly that risk —
see [`docs/company/cre8or-primer.md`](../../docs/company/cre8or-primer.md).
