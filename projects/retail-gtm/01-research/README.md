# Research plan

**Status:** ⚪ Not started.

What must be true for the channel model to work, and how we find out. Everything here
gets a source. Findings land in this folder; conclusions drawn from them belong in
`02-strategy/`.

## A. Internal — cheapest, do first

Most of these are answerable from documents and people we already have, and several
block the whole project. Do not start external research before closing A1–A3.

| # | Question | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | What are Cre8or's real unit economics at 12 / 500 / 5,000 / 50,000 units? | Price book, finance |
| A2 | Real lead times: concept → first shipment; reorder → restock | Ops |
| A3 | Product category range — what can we make in-house, what needs a third party? | Catalogs in `~/Attachments Cre8or/Brand Catalogs` |
| A4 | Capacity ceiling — concurrent partner volume the facilities can absorb | Ops |
| A5 | Geographic footprint and regulatory reach | "Global Presence Capabilities Overview" |
| A6 | What does *"The Cre8or & Agency Revolution"* already argue? | `~/Attachments Cre8or` |
| A7 | What partner/referral terms have been used before? | Referral & Finders Agreement templates |
| A8 | Named reference customers usable in a partner pitch | David |
| A9 | Prior partner conversations — who has been approached, what happened? | `Cre8or CRM.numbers` |

## B. Market — sizing the opportunity

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| B1 | How big is licensed / talent-fronted beauty, and how fast is it growing? |
| B2 | What share of tour and event merch is beauty vs. apparel vs. hard goods? |
| B3 | What are typical licensing royalty rates for beauty vs. apparel? |
| B4 | What do conventional cosmetic contract manufacturers require — MOQs, lead times, NRE? (This is the number the whole pitch is measured against.) |
| B5 | How much dead inventory does event-driven merch actually generate? Any public figures? |
| B6 | Which celebrity/creator beauty launches failed, and was inventory the cause? |

## C. Partners — one dossier per company

Use [`partners/_TEMPLATE.md`](partners/_TEMPLATE.md). Start with the segment leaders
named in [`../00-brief/channel-model.md`](../00-brief/channel-model.md).

For each: how they make money, whether they already do beauty and with whom, who owns
category expansion, their vendor onboarding process, and the specific event or launch
that would make a good first programme.

## D. Competitive — who else offers this

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| D1 | Which contract manufacturers already court licensors and agencies? |
| D2 | Are there white-label beauty platforms serving creators (e.g. Blanka-style dropship models)? How do they compare on quality and scale ceiling? |
| D3 | What do licensors do *today* when a client wants a beauty line? Who gets that call? |
| D4 | Where does Cre8or genuinely win, and where do we lose? Write the honest version. |

## E. Structural — the things that kill deals

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| E1 | MoCRA: who is the "responsible person" when product ships under a third party's brand? |
| E2 | International cosmetic compliance for a partner selling in multiple markets |
| E3 | Liability and insurance expectations of a major licensor |
| E4 | Typical licensor approval workflow and timeline for a new product category |
| E5 | Returns, recalls, and shelf-life obligations in a resale arrangement |

E1–E4 are the most likely reason a Segment A deal dies. Weight the effort accordingly.

## Method notes

- Prefer primary sources: partner filings, vendor portals, published rate cards.
- Every finding gets an entry in [`sources.md`](sources.md).
- Record what we *couldn't* find. Absence of evidence is itself a finding, and it stops
  the next person repeating the search.
