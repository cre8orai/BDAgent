# 2026-09-08 · The super agent

## What David asked for

Turn BDAgent into "multiple versions of me" — agents that log into LinkedIn in Chrome,
send InMails and email in his voice, run follow-up, stay on top of the inbox, and can
be sat on top of the way [tripleandco.com](https://www.tripleandco.com/) is structured.
Voice to come from scanning three years of sent mail. bizDave (his Lovable app) named
as the reference for the inbox half.

## What was built

**Six agents plus an orchestrator**, in `agents/`. Each is David in a different mode
rather than a generic assistant with a title: Scout (who is worth a call), Ghost (the
voice), Opener (first touch), Chaser (follow-up), Desk (inbound), Closer (money and
next step). Chief sits above them and is who David talks to.

Structure follows Triple & Co's model — a human at the apex, an orchestrator below,
specialists beneath, **zero unsupervised output** — but the agents are named for the
David they are, since that is what he asked for.

**The voice, from real evidence.** `voice/STYLE.md`, built from 287 of David's own sent
messages (Feb 2025 – Sep 2026, 100 external read in full or in opening). Every rule is
backed by a sentence he actually wrote. The findings that matter most:

- His greeting is the recipient's **name on its own line** — `Jesse hello`, `Nate hello`,
  `Noah`. Not "Hi X,". `Hi <Name>,` is his *formal* register and marks a substantive
  message.
- `Let's Cre8!` closes essentially everything.
- Follow-ups are 10–25 words and his verb is **bump**, never "circle back".
- The ellipsis is his connective tissue: `Eric... I will put you in BCC :)`
- He always thanks the introducer by name in the first two lines, then BCCs them.
- He never closes on "let me know" — always a Calendly link, a named day, or a person.
- **He does not pitch in a first message.** He offers to explain on a call. The Noah /
  BeautySpace email is 43 words and contains no pitch at all.

`voice/ingest.py` turns raw Gmail JSON into a clean corpus; the corpus and raw mail are
gitignored and only the derived guide is committed.

**The gate, which is the actual design.** `agents/GATES.md`. No agent has a send tool.
Agents write to `agents/state/outbox.csv` with `status=draft` and stop. `send.sh` is
the only thing that transmits, refuses to run without a TTY, and handles only rows
David marked `approved`. Two permission profiles enforce it in code: the unattended
`.claude/settings.json` puts Gmail's send tools and browser click tools in `deny`;
`.claude/settings.send.json` allows them and is used by `send.sh` alone.

LinkedIn is capped at 8/session, 40–90s apart, 1 session/day, and any captcha stops the
run rather than retrying.

**State seeded from real threads**, not invented: Front Row (two follow-ups, no reply,
escalation candidate), Barrel VC (materials promised "shortly" on Sept 3, now overdue),
BeautySpace, IWorld. Two overdue commitments were found by reading what David actually
wrote — `"I'll get them to you shortly"`, `"I will revert tomorrow!"`.

**`pipeline.html`** is regenerated by `build_dashboard.py` after every run.

**`CONNECT.md`** answers the enablement question directly.

## Decided

- **BDAgent does not duplicate RetailGTM.** RetailGTM sources and scores *accounts*;
  BDAgent starts where it stops — turning a qualified account into a conversation with
  a named person. Scout here reads `RetailGTM/agents/state/qualified.csv`.
- **Ghost is a filter, not an author.** It owns how things sound; Opener/Chaser/Desk/
  Closer own what is said. It has the right to send a draft back.
- **Four follow-up touches, then stop.** Message five gets a reputation, not a reply.
- **Sending is never automated**, for three separate reasons that are each sufficient.
